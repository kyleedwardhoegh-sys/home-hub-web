What to expect

Dinner is served at 5:00 PM and volunteers are welcome to join. Ideally we have at least 2 adults in each room, and the more the merrier.

  • 5:30–5:40: Meet at the youth registration (slides) area. Check younger children in to the nursery, then circle up with the older kids and review the rules.
  • 5:40–6:40: Playground (weather permitting) and/or gym.
  • 6:40–7:00: Watch a show downstairs.
  • 7:00–7:15: Play in the foosball room if there's time, otherwise the slides until pick-up.

Questions or new to volunteering? Contact Kyle Hoegh (text 507-398-2669, email) or Emily Reesnes, Calvary Kids Director (email).
